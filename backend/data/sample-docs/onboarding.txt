ACME NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING

Week 1: Setup
On your first day, you will receive a laptop and accounts for email, Slack, and the internal wiki. Complete the security training within your first 3 days. Your manager will schedule a welcome meeting and introduce you to your team.

Working Hours and Leave
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Full-time employees receive 21 days of paid annual leave, 10 days of sick leave, and public holidays. Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal and approved by your manager.

Expenses
Work-related expenses under 100 USD do not need pre-approval. Larger expenses need manager approval before purchase. Submit receipts through the HR portal within 30 days to be reimbursed.

Equipment
Employees may choose a laptop from the approved list. Equipment must be returned within 7 days of leaving the company. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT immediately.

Support Rotation
Support engineers join the on-call rotation after their first month. On-call shifts last one week, with a handover meeting every Monday at 09:30.
